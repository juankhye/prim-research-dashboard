# Primoris Services (PRIM) — First-Principles Research Dashboard

Interactive single-page hedge-fund-quality research dashboard for **Primoris Services Corporation (NYSE: PRIM)** — a $5.7B-mkt-cap specialty infrastructure contractor that builds power lines, gas pipelines, solar farms, and data center electrical rooms across the US and Canada.

**Live site:** https://juankhye.github.io/prim-research-dashboard/

## What's inside

- **Snapshot** — The 50% Q1 2026 drop in one chart; live KPI counters; broker price-target spread
- **What is PRIM?** — First-principles intro to the contractor business model, segments, leadership
- **Business Model** — Self-perform moat, MSA vs project work, book-to-bill mechanics
- **EPC 101** — Glossary of the 8 terms you need (EPC, MSA, eBOS, peaker, OBBBA, etc.)
- **Geographic Footprint** — Interactive US state map with HQ + Energy/Utilities operating presence
- **Revenue & Cost Drivers** — Bar charts of what cranks revenue vs. what chews up margin
- **Financials** — 2018-2028E revenue/EBITDA/EPS history with quarterly Energy & Utilities GM% trend
- **Backlog & Bookings** — Quarterly backlog by segment from 1Q16-4Q25 (40 quarters), book-to-bill warning signal
- **The Q1 Disaster** — Anatomy of the 50% drop, $80M EBITDA cut bridge, 6 troubled-project completion timeline
- **End Markets / Megatrends** — Data center, gas-gen super-cycle, grid modernization
- **PayneCrest M&A** — $399.5M deal mechanics, strategic logic, multiple arbitrage
- **Valuation** — Peer comp (PWR, MTZ, EME, MYRG, DY), broker price targets (Goldman to Needham)
- **Bull vs Bear** — 8 points on each side of the debate
- **Risks** — 8 ranked risks with severity meters
- **Catalysts & Timeline** — Forward catalysts and recent timeline
- **Glossary** — Quick-reference

## Tech

Single static `index.html`. Chart.js for charts. Inline SVG US map (no external tile API). No backend, no build step.
